# Responsible use

Lion AI Studios can clone voices, put a chosen face into new video, and re-shoot existing Shorts. These are powerful and
easy to misuse. The studio builds in a few guard rails; the rest is on whoever runs it.

**Built in**
- **Consent checkbox**: *Cast* and *Recreate a Short* will not start until you confirm that you have the right to use
  the person's likeness (yourself, someone who consented, or a fictional or licensed character). Voice cloning asks the same.
- **No performer description**: when the director re-describes a Short, it is told not to describe the original
  performer's face, hair, skin, age or ethnicity. The look comes only from the reference image you supply. In the *Faithful*
  style the director writes a short visual description (clothing, hair, distinctive features, never a name) only to find the
  performer in each frame; the new face still comes only from your reference image.
- **Localhost by default**: the app listens on 127.0.0.1. Put an authenticating reverse proxy in front before exposing it.

**Your responsibility**
- Do not impersonate real people, create sexual or deceptive content of real people, or make anyone appear to say or do
  something they did not, without their explicit consent.
- Respect copyright and platform terms. Downloading or remaking someone else's video, music or voice may need the
  rights holder's permission. *Recreate a Short* is meant for your own videos and content you have rights to.
  Its *original music* and *original audio* soundtrack options reuse the Short's own recording, so publishing the result may need a licence for that music.
- Label AI-generated media where people could mistake it for real footage.
- Follow each model's licence and acceptable-use policy (see [MODELS.md](MODELS.md)).
